## What We've Fixed After Listening

We don't cherry-pick reviews. The low-star feedback clusters in a few honest areas, and we've acted on them:

- **Manuals**: buyers asked for clearer, multi-language instructions — new production batches ship with updated guides.
- **Packaging & accessories**: feedback about missing parts and fragile glass pitchers led to new packaging checks and a clearer included-parts list on every product page.
- **Delivery communication**: shipping delays were the top frustration. We've added earlier proactive updates so you hear about delays before you have to ask.
- **Quality control**: defect reports on a small number of units — from steam function to grind dials — are routed straight to QC review and the affected batches were re-inspected.
