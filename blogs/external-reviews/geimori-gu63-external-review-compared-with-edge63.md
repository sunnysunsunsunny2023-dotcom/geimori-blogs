# What an External Reviewer Found About the Geimori GU63
Morning Coffee independently compared the Geimori GU63 with the Starseeker Edge63. We reviewed the findings to understand where GU63 stood out, where the comparison grinder had an advantage, and what the results mean for everyday GU63 users.
**External review source:** Morning Coffee, *“Same Size Burrs, VERY Different Grinders!”*, published September 12, 2026. The measurements and opinions below are attributed to that third-party review unless otherwise noted. Current GU63 product information is used only where present-day official specifications are more appropriate.
Where GU63 stood out
### A quieter, lighter, lower-fuss daily workflow
The reviewer highlighted lower measured noise, lower weight, practical low-retention performance, an easier-to-handle dosing cup, and a lower price in the comparison.
Where the other grinder stood out
### Finer stepped control and easier setting preservation
The comparison grinder offered smaller stepped adjustments and a cleaning design that preserved the visible grind setting after disassembly.
**Review takeaway:** the external test did not show that one grinder can make good coffee while the other cannot. The bigger difference was workflow: GU63 made a particularly strong case for users who value quiet operation, low fuss and everyday usability.

## 1. Where GU63 stood out: quieter and easier to live with
Two of the clearest differences in the review were noise and weight. GU63 measured around 52 dB, compared with 56–58 dB for the comparison grinder, and the reviewer put GU63 at roughly 3.0 kg versus about 4.6 kg.
Those numbers matter because grinders live on real kitchen counters. A quieter grinder can be easier to use early in the morning or in a shared space, while a lighter grinder is easier to move when counter space is limited.
**What the review suggests:** GU63’s advantage is not just a spec-sheet number. Its lower noise and lower weight directly support a calmer everyday routine.
## 2. Dialing in: enough control without making every adjustment a project
The comparison grinder offered finer stepped changes, which the reviewer saw as useful for users who enjoy very small, repeatable click adjustments.
GU63 takes a different approach with stepless adjustment. The source reviewer described using a relatively compact part of its available range for normal coffee, and argued that having fewer decisions can actually make dialing in easier.
**What the review suggests:** the comparison grinder may appeal more to users who enjoy micro-adjusting. GU63 may appeal more to people who want control without turning the grinder into the main event.
## 3. One area where the reviewer preferred the comparison grinder: cleaning calibration
The external review gave the comparison grinder a meaningful advantage when it came to preserving the selected grind setting after cleaning. Its upper section could be removed and reinstalled while the visible setting remained in place.
GU63 also provides tool-free access, but the reviewer noted that users should remember their previous setting and return the adjustment to that position after reassembly.
We include this because an external review is most useful when it shows both strengths and trade-offs. For people who deep-clean frequently, setting preservation can be a real convenience.
## 4. In the cup: GU63 produced the reviewer’s preferred initial espresso result
Using the same espresso machine and puck-preparation approach, the reviewer reported a GU63 shot of about 30 seconds with a balanced, full-bodied impression. The initial shot from the comparison grinder ran faster, at about 22 seconds, and was described as lighter.
That does not mean the comparison grinder could not produce a strong espresso. The reviewer specifically noted that moving it finer could bring the extraction toward 30 seconds and create a fuller cup.
**The useful finding:** both grinders were capable. In this particular test, GU63 reached a satisfying espresso result with less adjustment.
## 5. Retention and day-to-day cleanup were another strong point for GU63
The reviewer used GU63 as a regular daily grinder and described the interior as very clean. In the example discussed in the review, an 18 g dose produced close to 18 g out, with only a small amount remaining and then clearing on the next grind or with the bellows.
Retention will always vary with beans, humidity, grind size and technique. What matters here is the third-party reviewer’s practical impression: GU63 required relatively little day-to-day cleanup.
## What this external review means for GU63 buyers
This review is most useful when viewed as an independent snapshot of how GU63 behaves in a real home-barista workflow. The strongest case for GU63 was not that it “beat” another grinder on every dimension. It was that the combination of lower noise, lower weight, practical retention, straightforward dialing and lower price made it easy to live with.
If you value very fine stepped adjustment or setting preservation after disassembly, the comparison grinder had legitimate advantages. If you care more about a quieter, simpler, lower-fuss single-dose routine, the external review made a strong case for GU63.
