## GU38 Buyer FAQ
Is the Geimori GU38 a good grinder for daily home brewing?
GU38 is designed for home users who want a compact electric grinder for everyday coffee.
          It is a good fit if you want a small single-dose grinder for daily brewing without taking up much counter space.
Can GU38 be used for espresso and pour-over?
GU38 is built to cover a wide range of grind settings, so shoppers often compare it for espresso, pour-over,
          drip coffee, and other home brewing methods. For espresso, the best result still depends on your beans,
          recipe, machine, and dialing-in process.
Who should choose GU38 instead of GU63?
Choose GU38 if you want a more compact, space-saving grinder for daily home use. Consider GU63 if you want
          a larger grinder with a more advanced home single-dose workflow and stronger focus on controlled grinding.
Is GU38 portable like T38?
No. GU38 is a plug-in home grinder. If you need battery-powered grinding for travel, camping, or outdoor use,
          compare GU38 with the T38 portable grinder instead.
