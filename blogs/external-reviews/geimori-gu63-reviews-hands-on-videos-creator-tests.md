## What the Reviews Examine
Each creator approaches the GU63 differently, but the reviews generally
      focus on several questions that matter when choosing a home coffee grinder.
#### Espresso Dialing
Espresso requires small and controlled grind adjustments.
          Reviewers examine how easily the GU63 can be adjusted while
          dialing in different beans and espresso recipes.
#### Grind Quality
The reviews consider how the GU63’s 63mm conical burr platform
          performs for espresso and filter coffee, including consistency
          and the character of the resulting cup.
#### Single-Dose Workflow
A single-dose workflow allows users to weigh and grind one serving
          at a time, making it easier to change beans or alternate between
          espresso and filter coffee.
#### Retention and Cleanliness
Reviewers look at how much ground coffee remains inside the grinder
          and whether the plasma anti-static system helps reduce loose grounds
          around the dosing area.
#### Build and Usability
Adjustment feel, noise, controls, dosing-cup workflow, cleaning,
          counter footprint, and construction all affect how enjoyable
          a grinder is to use every day.
#### Overall Value
Several reviewers consider whether the GU63 offers a competitive
          combination of burr size, adjustment control, workflow, and build
          quality at its price.
