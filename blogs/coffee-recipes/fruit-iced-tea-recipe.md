Hot weather calls for a refreshing glass of ice water! If this glass of ice water could choose another flavor, what flavor would you like it? We understand that making a glass of iced tea can be time-consuming. You need to brew the tea first and then chill it in the refrigerator or cool it with ice cubes before you can enjoy it.
Want to get a glass of iced tea quickly? Ice coffee maker can make it happen. It can brew both iced tea and coffee. The principle of making it is the same: just put the tea leaves and tea bags, and coffee in the filter basket, and you can get different ice drink.
**How to Make Fruit Ice Tea?**
- 4 regular black tea bags or 4 tablespoons tea
- ½ cup mixed berries or fruit
- ½ Sugar or syrup
- Ice cubes
- Wirsh Iced coffee Maker
Note: Frozen or fresh fruit can be used
1. Place the berry puree or chopped fruit in a serving cup.
1. Place 4 tea bags or 4 tablespoons of tea leaves in a strainer basket and pour 5 cups of water into the water tank, being careful not to exceed the Max line.
1. Add ice and syrup to the coffee pot, add more if you like it sweeter.
1. Start the machine and adjust the strength of the tea.
1. Wait until the iced tea is cold enough, pour it into the serving cup, and stir well, then enjoy!
**How to Make Southern Sweet Tea?**
- 4 Southern tea bags
- ½ Cup Sugar or syrup
- Ice cubes
- Wirsh Iced Tea Maker
1. Place 4 southern tea bags in the filter basket, making sure the lid is closed.
1. Add 5 cups of water to the water tank (no more than the Max line)
1. Add enough ice cubes, lemon slices and pour 6 teaspoons of sugar or syrup into the coffee pot.
1. Turn on the machine and adjust the strength of the tea.
1. Once the tea has filled the coffee pot, take it out, stir it, and pour it into your cup.
Note: If the tea is too weak or too sweet, adding more simple syrup and brewing additional tea bags can solve the issue.
