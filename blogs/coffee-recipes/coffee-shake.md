**Ingredient list**
200 ml cold milk (1.5% fat)
20 g protein powder
vanilla
1 double espresso
optional ice cubes
**preparation**
Brew single or double of fresh espresso with Wirsh espresso machine.
Put all the ingredients in a blender and mix thoroughly - done!
