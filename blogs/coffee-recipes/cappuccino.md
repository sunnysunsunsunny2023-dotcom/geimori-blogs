An espresso with ⅓ steamed milk, topped with ⅓ creamy foam and coca powder.
**Ingredient**
- Cup (6-8 oz)
- Single or double shot of espresso (1-2 oz)
- ⅓ Frothed milk, ⅓ foam
- 1 tsp cocoa powder
**Preparation**
Brew one shot of fresh espresso with Wirsh espresso machine**.**
Pour the frothed milk to the freshly brewed espresso
Add the cocoa powder in the top of milk foam
Enjoy!
