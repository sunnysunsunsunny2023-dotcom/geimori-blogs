**Caramel Crunch Latte**
Ingredients:
Double shot of espresso
1 cup of milk
2 tablespoons of caramel sauce
Crunchy caramel bits
Making Steps:
1. make a shot of espresso using a Wirsh coffee maker.
1. Steam the milk in a coffee maker until a fine froth forms.
1. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso to form a latte.
1. Drizzle caramel sauce over the top of the coffee and sprinkle with crunchy caramel pieces.
**Gingerbread Cookie Latte**
Ingredients:
1 espresso
1 cup milk
2 tablespoons gingerbread syrup
Gingerbread cookie crumbs
Making Steps:
1. make an espresso using a Wirsh coffee maker.
1. Steam the milk in the coffee maker until a fine froth forms.
1. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso to form a latte.
1. Add the gingerbread syrup and stir well.
1. Sprinkle some gingerbread cookie crumbs on top of the coffee.
**Orange Americano**
Ingredients:
1 cup of Americano
Shredded orange peel
1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
Making Steps:
1. make an Americano using a Wirsh coffee maker.
1. Add shredded orange peel, cinnamon and ground cloves to the coffee and stir well.
