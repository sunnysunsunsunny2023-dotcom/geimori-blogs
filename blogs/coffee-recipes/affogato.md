**Ingredient**
2 heaped spoons of vanilla ice cream
1 double espresso
Chopped pistachios or grated chocolate (optional)
**Preparation**
Brew a double espresso with wirsh espresso machine.
Scoop ice-cream into glass
Pour espresso directly over ice-cream.
Garnish with chopped pistachios or grated chocolate.
Enjoy!
