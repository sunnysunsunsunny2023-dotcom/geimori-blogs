Iced coffee -the popular summer drink now is made simple and without dilution!
**Preparation**
Brew coffee from a Wirsh drip coffee maker directly to the wirsh instant coffee chiller.
Swirl the chiller up and in one minute the hot coffee will become cold.
Top each glass with cream, milk, syrups or sweeteners as desired.
Enjoy!
