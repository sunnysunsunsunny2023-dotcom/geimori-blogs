If you're a coffee lover with a penchant for indulgent treats, then you're in for a delectable surprise. We have the perfect recipe for a homemade S'Mores Latte that will satisfy your cravings. The great thing about this s'mores latte is that it's reminiscent of those cold fall nights. With the Wirsh 20 Bar Espresso Machine, you can easily create café-quality beverages in the comfort of your own home. So, let's get started on this irresistible S'Mores Latte journey!
**What You****’****ll Need**
- 2shot of espresso (using the Wirsh 20-Bar Espresso Machine)
- 1 cup of 2% milk (your preferred choice)
- 2 tablespoons of chocolate syrup
- 2 tablespoons of marshmallow fluff
- 1 tablespoon of crushed cracker squares
- Whipped cream (optional, for garnish)
- Mini marshmallows (optional, for garnish)
**Instructions:**
1. Prepare the Wirsh 20Bar Espresso Machine
- Set up and preheat the espresso machine.
- Grind your favorite coffee beans to a fine consistency.
- Insert the portafilter into the machine and lock it securely.
- Place your cup underneath the portafilter.
- Start the brewing process and allow the Wirsh Espresso Machine to extract a single shot of rich, aromatic espresso.
1. Spread the crushed graham crackers in a circle on a small plate, coat the chocolate syrup along the rim of the latte cup, then dip the rim into the graham cracker crumbs.
1. Pour the shot of espresso into the cupwith the melted marshmallow, chocolate syrup, and graham crackers. Stir gently to combine the ingredients.
1. Use the steam wandto steam the milk and foam until you see a layer of foam forming at the top. slowly pour the steamed milk in the center to fill the mug and add a thin layer of the foam to the top.
1. Garnish and serve:If desired, top your S'Mores Latte with a dollop of whipped cream, mini marshmallows, and a sprinkle of crushed graham crackers. Drizzle with remaining chocolate syrup.
1. Grab a spoon, find a cozy spot, and enjoy your homemade S'Mores Latte!
This S'Mores Latte recipe combines the irresistible flavors of chocolate, marshmallow, and graham crackers with the richness of espresso, resulting in a decadent treat that will transport you to a cozy campfire. So, indulge in this delightful coffee drink with your Wirsh Espresso Machine.
