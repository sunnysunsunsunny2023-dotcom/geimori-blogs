Looking to beat the summer heat with a delicious and refreshing cold drink? Why not try making homemade mixed berry iced tea! This article will guide you through the simple steps of creating a cup of flavorful mixed berry iced tea that will keep you cool all in summer day.
**What are the benefits of Mixed Berry Iced Tea to health?**
Mixed berry iced tea offers numerous health benefits, making it an excellent choice for a summer beverage. Berries like blueberries, blackberries, raspberries, and other berries are packed with powerful antioxidants and abundant vitamins that can ward off disease and reduce cancer risk. Additionally, black tea, which is the base of this delightful drink, is rich in polyphenols, which are also powerful antioxidants. So when a variety of berries are combined with black tea, you get a drink rich in free radical fighting antioxidants.
**What you will need to make Mixed Berry Iced Tea?**
- 6 regular black tea bags
- 1 cup mixed berries (blueberries, blackberries, raspberries or any other preferred berries)
- ¼ cup sugar or sweetener(adjust to taste)
- Iced cubes
- [Wirsh Iced Tea Maker](https://mywirsh.com/products/wirsh-iced-coffee-maker-iced-tea-maker-with-85-ounce-pitcher-strength-control-and-reusable-filter)
**How to make Mixed Berry Iced Tea?**
1. Wash and dry the berries.
1. Open the water tank lid of the Wirsh Iced Tea Makerand fill it with drinking water. Making sure not to exceed the MAX level indicated on the water tank.
1. Place6 black tea bags into the filter basket and then close the water tank lid completely.
1. Add some ice cubesand not exceed the MAX level indicated on the water tank.
1. Gently mash the berries and add themto the glass pitcher.
1. Press the "On/0ff" keyon your tea maker, its corresponding indicator illuminates in blue, the appliance starts to work.
1. Brew tea into the pitcher, stir well, and enjoy!
Note:
- The strength and sweetness of the tea can be adjusted according to personal taste.
- It's best to drink your homemade mixed berryiced tea immediately after brewing for the best taste.
What better way to get your daily dose of antioxidants and vitamins than a freshly brewed mixed berry iced tea? Quench your thirst and boost your health with a glass of freshly brewed mixed berry iced tea this summer.
