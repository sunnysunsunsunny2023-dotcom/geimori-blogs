Pumpkin spice Latte is a popular fall drink that combines the rich flavors of espresso, pumpkin, and warm spices. Instead of heading to your local coffee shop, why not try making your own PSL at home using the Wirsh 20 bar Espresso Machine? Let's dive into this easy-to-follow recipe and enjoy the cozy flavors of fall!
**Pumpkin Spice Latte Ingredients**
- 1 shot of espresso (using the Wirsh 20-Bar Espresso Machine)
- 1 cup of milk (any type - whole, skim, almond, etc.)
- 2 tablespoons of pumpkin puree
- 1 tablespoon of sugar (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 teaspoon of pumpkin pie spice
- Whipped cream (optional)
- Cinnamon powder (for garnish)
**How to Make**
1. Prepare the espresso using the Wirsh 20 Bar Espresso Machine. Make sure you have a rich and strong shot of espresso.
1. In a small saucepan, combine the milk, pumpkin puree, sugar, and pumpkin pie spice. Heat mixture over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until heated.
1. Heat and froth milk until it becomes creamy and foamy.
1. Pour the freshly brewed espresso shot into a mug.
1. Slowly pour the frothed milk over the espresso, holding back the foam with a spoon to create a layered effect.
1. Top the latte with a dollop of whipped cream (if desired) and sprinkle with a pinch of cinnamon for added flavor and garnish.
1. Serve immediately and enjoy the comfortable taste of your homemade pumpkin spice latte!
**Tips:**
If you don't have pumpkin pie spice, you can make your own with cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cloves.
- 6 cinnamon sticks
- 1 tsp ground cloves
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 2 tsp ground nutmeg
Enjoy the cozy flavors of fall with this homemade pumpkin spice latte recipe. By using the Wirsh 20 Bar Espresso Machine, you can get the perfect espresso, creating a delightful latte that satisfies your craving for a warm and comforting drink. So grab your favorite mug and follow these simple steps to make a delicious pumpkin spice latte!
