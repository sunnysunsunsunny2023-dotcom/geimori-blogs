FAQ
## Common Questions
### Is the T38 Plus good for beginners?
Yes, the T38 Plus can be a good upgrade for beginners who are ready to move beyond basic grinders. A better grinder can make a major difference in cup quality because grind consistency affects extraction. If your coffee tastes too bitter, too sour, or inconsistent from day to day, the grinder may be one of the reasons.
### Is the T38 Plus good for espresso?
The T38 Plus is designed to support espresso grinding, especially for home users who want stepless adjustment and more control over grind size. Espresso can be demanding because small grind changes can affect extraction time and flavor. A grinder with precise adjustment gives you more control when dialing in your shot.
### Is the T38 Plus good for pour-over?
Yes, the T38 Plus can also be used for filter coffee and pour-over brewing. For many users, this flexibility is one of the main reasons to choose it. If you brew with methods such as V60, Chemex, drip coffee, or other filter-style recipes, having adjustable grind control can help you fine-tune sweetness, clarity, and balance.
