**Apricot Iced Coffee Recipe**
Mentioned below is how you can prepare an iced coffee recipe.
**What you need**
- ¾ cup of chilled coffee
- ½ cup of chilled apricot juice
- ½ pint of softened ice cream (vanilla feature)
- ½ cup fresh apricots
**How to prepare it?**
- First, you should mix all the ingredients (coffee, apricot nectar and milk) in an appropriate bowl.
- Now you should add the ice cream which is already softened. Then, beat the mixture so it becomes smooth.
- Then, blend the fresh apricots using a blender. Be sure that you make it smooth. Then, pour this smoothened apricot mixture to a glass. After that, top it with the mixture.

**Coffee Punch**
Here’s how to make a Coffee Punch.
**What you need**
- 2 qt. of softened vanilla ice cream
- 2 qt. of strong brewed chilled coffee
- 2 cups milk
- 2 cups whipping cream
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- Ground nutmeg
**How to prepare it?**
- First, you should combine coffee, sugar, milk and vanilla. Stir the mixture thoroughly and let it chill.
- Put the softened vanilla ice cream in a sizeable punch bowl.
- Pour the entire mixture on top of the ice cream and stir it gently.
- Top it using whipped cream and sprinkle some nutmeg before serving.
**Mocha Coffee**
Cake and a hot cup of a tasty Mocha Coffee are a great way to start the day with the whole family. Serve immediately from a thermal carafe or keep warm in a stovetop warming pot.
**What you need**
- 14 oz of milk (evaporated)
- 2 cups strong blended coffee
- 1 cup hot water
- ½ cup cocoa powder
- ½ cup sugar
**How to prepare it?**
- Use a saucepan to mix sugar and cocoa powder together.
- Add some hot water and whisk it gently to make it smooth
- Heat the mixture gently while stirring for 2 minutes so it will turn into a thick mixture.
- Now, add some evaporated milk to the coffee
- Heat until it boils and serve
