## What is a WDT Tool?
The WDT(Weiss Distribution Technique)tool is a simple but powerful accessory used in espresso preparation.It typically consists of a handle with multiple fine needles(typically made of stainless steel). The goal? To break up clumps and evenly redistribute coffee grounds in the portafilter before tamping. This helps reduce channeling—those pesky water paths that cause uneven extraction—and leads to better-tasting espresso.
## Why You Need a WDT Tool for Better Espresso?
Espresso extraction relies heavily on even water flow through compacted coffee grounds. However, when you grind coffee, especially when using fine grinds, the grounds can clump together due to static or the natural behavior of ground coffee. These clumps can lead to channeling, where water finds the path of least resistance and causes uneven extraction.
- Here's where the WDT tool shines:
Eliminates Clumping - Coffee grounds often clump due to static or moisture, leading to uneven extraction. WDT breaks up these clumps, ensuring consistent extraction.
- Prevents Channeling - Channeling happens when water finds the path of least resistance, bypassing some coffee. This results in under-extracted (sour) and over-extracted (bitter) flavors in the same shot. WDT tool ensures even distribution of grounds in the portafilter, reducing channeling risks.
- Improves Espresso Quality & Consistency - A well-distributed puck yields more balanced flavors and better repeatability.
- Better Workflow - It’s an affordable and easy-to-use small espresso accessory that can significantly boost the quality of your shots.

## How to Choose the Best WDT Tool?

Thinner needles (0.2–0.4mm) work best for espresso-fine grounds.
[if !supportLists]2.[endif]Number of Needles (3–9 is Common)
