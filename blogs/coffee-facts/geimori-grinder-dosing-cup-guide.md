What's included with your grinder, why dosing cups are also sold separately, and how to choose the right replacement.
THE SHORT ANSWER

Wondering whether you need to add a dosing cup to your order? You don't.
Every Geimori coffee grinder includes a dosing cup in its standard package.
There is no need to purchase one separately to get started.
That applies to the GU63, GU64, GU64 Gen2, GU38, T38 Plus, T38 Battery, and T38 Gen2. Each model comes with a dosing cup suited to its design.

DAILY WORKFLOW
## What is the dosing cup for?
A dosing cup collects ground coffee after grinding and makes it easier to transfer grounds into your brewing setup. In a single-dose routine, weigh your beans, grind them, and move the grounds from the cup to your portafilter or other brewing equipment.
The included cup is part of the grinder's everyday workflow—not an accessory you need to buy before your first use.
ACCESSORIES
## Why can I also buy a dosing cup separately?
Extra dosing cups are optional. You might want a spare, a replacement, or an additional cup when preparing different beans.
Before ordering an extra cup, check compatibility with your exact grinder model. Similar diameter does not guarantee identical fit or attachment design.

### Does the Geimori GU63 come with a dosing cup?
Yes. A dosing cup is included in the standard GU63 package. You do not need to buy one separately.

Yes. Both models include a dosing cup. Choose model-specific accessories if you need an additional cup.
### Do the GU38 and T38 grinders include dosing cups?
Yes. The GU38, T38 Plus, T38 Battery, and T38 Gen2 all come with a dosing cup.

No. The included cup collects ground coffee. Check your brewing equipment specifications for fit and transfer preferences.
