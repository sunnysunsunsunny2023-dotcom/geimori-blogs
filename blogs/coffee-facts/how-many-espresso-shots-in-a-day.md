Coffee lovers and those interested in caffeine often wonder how many cups of espresso they can drink in a day. Next, we'll discuss daily coffee consumption, coffee-drinking benefits, and the dangers of overdrinking.
**Benefits of Drinking Espresso Shot**
Espresso coffees offer more than just a caffeine boost. They are rich in antioxidants and essential nutrients that contribute to overall health. Studies have shown that the antioxidants in espresso can help reduce inflammation, prevent certain chronic diseases, and support cardiovascular health. Additionally, the high levels of caffeine found in espresso may improve cognitive function, increase alertness, and boost energy levels.
**Daily Espresso Limit**
Recommended daily intake varies depending on a number of factors, including age, overall health, and caffeine sensitivity. The usual expert recommendation is to drink as little as 1-3 cups per day. According to the FDA, it is recommended not to consume more than 400 mg of caffeine per day.
