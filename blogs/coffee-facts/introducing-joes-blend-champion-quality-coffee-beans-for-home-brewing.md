Wirsh proudly announces the launch of their latest coffee product, **Wirsh Joe's Blend**.
Wirsh and Joe's collaboration in launching Joe's Blend was aimed at providing coffee lovers with premium coffee beans that can be enjoyed at home without breaking the bank. This initiative aligns perfectly with Wirsh's commitment to providing quality, sustainability and affordability specialty coffee experiences to all coffee enthusiasts.
At the heart of Wirsh Joe's Blend is the expertise of Joe Yang, the master roaster behind this exceptional creation. With 12 years of experience in the coffee industry, Joe's remarkable achievements speak volumes about his talent and expertise. In 2023, he was **crowned the US Brewer's Cup Champion**, showcasing his dedication and unparalleled skills. Additionally, Joe secured**the runner-up position in the 2023 US Latte Art Competition** and ranked among **the top 6 best roasters in the 2022 US Roasting Competition**. His expertise ensures that Joe's Blend delivers a top-notch flavor profile.
Each batch of Joe's Blend is carefully selected from the finest coffee beans. The beans are then roasted in small batches to maintain the freshness of the beans, ensuring that their full flavour potential is released when extracted, resulting in a harmonious balance of aroma, acidity and body. These champion quality coffee beans have been meticulously roasted to perfection, allowing you to enjoy specialty coffee in the comfort of your own homes.
Ready to indulge in the champion-quality taste? Wirsh Joe's Blend is now available for purchase on our site.
