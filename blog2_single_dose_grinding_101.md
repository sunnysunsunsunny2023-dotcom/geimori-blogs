# Single-Dose Grinding 101: Why It Makes Your Morning Coffee Better

What single-dose grinding is, why it matters, and how to do it right — based on how real customers use their grinders.

## What "Single Dose" Actually Means

A single-dose grinder holds no beans between uses. You weigh out exactly what you need for one brew, grind it, and the grinder is empty again. That's it — but the effects are bigger than they sound.

## Why It Changes Your Coffee

- **Freshness**: ground coffee loses aroma fast. Grinding only what you need means every cup starts from whole, fresh beans.
- **No stale mix**: with a hopper, old grounds sit and get pushed out hours later. Single-dose means what you grind is what you brew.
- **Freedom to switch**: drink espresso in the morning and decaf at night? Change beans any time without emptying a hopper.
- **Less waste**: you dose by weight, not by guess. No more over-grinding and dumping the extra.

## The One Tip Our Customers Swear By: Use the Bellows

A common question we get is "why is my grinder holding onto grounds?" A little retention is normal — static and burr geometry trap a small amount of coffee. The fix is already in the box: the bellows.

Here's what a verified GU63 owner told us: "There is a lot of retention if the included bellows is not used. A few pumps and I get most of the grinds out. I'm happy with it."

The routine takes three seconds: pump the bellows 3-4 times after grinding, and you'll push nearly every particle into your dosing cup.

## How to Dial In a New Bag of Beans

- Start at the grind size you used for the last bag that tasted good.
- Pull a shot or brew a cup. Too fast and sour? Grind finer. Too slow and bitter? Grind coarser.
- Change one variable at a time, and only one step per attempt.
- Log what worked. In two or three tries you'll be in the zone — that's normal, even for pros.

## Should You Clean Between Bean Changes?

Switching from a dark roast to a light roast? Run a few grams of the new beans through first, or brush the burr chamber — especially if you're tasting yesterday's flavors. You don't need a full teardown; a quick brush and a purge dose keeps flavors clean.
